# SkillSync AI

> AI-Powered Career Companion for Students
---

## Problem

Students often:

- Learn random skills
- Don't know internship requirements
- Lack a structured roadmap
- Have no progress tracking

---

## Solution

SkillSync AI helps students by:

- Resume Analysis
- Skill Gap Detection
- Personalized Roadmaps
- AI Career Mentor
- Progress Tracking
- Internship Preparation

---
