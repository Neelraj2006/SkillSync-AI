# SkillSync AI Vision

## Mission

Help students become internship-ready through personalized AI guidance.

---

## Problem

Most students learn without direction.

They struggle with:

- Skill selection
- Career planning
- Resume building
- Internship preparation

---

## Our Solution

SkillSync AI provides:

- AI Career Mentor
- Resume Analysis
- Skill Gap Detection
- Personalized Learning Roadmap
- Progress Tracking
- Interview Preparation

---

## Target Users

- Students
- Freshers
- Internship Seekers

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the all-in-one AI career platform for students.

---

## Success Metric

A student should always know:

> **What should I learn next?**
